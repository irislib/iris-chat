Iris Chat — Privacy

Last updated: 28 April 2026

What we collect

Iris Chat does not ask for your name, email, or phone number. Your identity is a keypair generated on your device. We do not maintain user accounts.

What stays on your device

Your private key, contacts, group memberships, and message history are stored locally on the devices you use Iris Chat on. They are not uploaded to us.

What goes to the Nostr network

To deliver messages, Iris Chat publishes encrypted events to public Nostr relays you choose (for example relay.damus.io, nos.lol). Direct messages are wrapped with rotating keys that change per message, so relay operators do not see who is sending or receiving them — only encrypted payloads and timing metadata. If you choose to publish a public profile (display name, picture, bio), that event is visible to relays under your public key.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, your device's Apple Push Notification token is sent to a notification relay so messages addressed to you can wake the app. The relay sees only encrypted payloads, never message contents.

No tracking, no analytics

Iris Chat does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or behavioural tracking.

Your data, your control

You can erase all local data by uninstalling the app. To remove published events from a Nostr relay, send a deletion event from the same key, or contact the relay operator. We do not control or operate any specific relay.
